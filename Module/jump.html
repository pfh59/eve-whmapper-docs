Jump

WHMapper records every jump through a connection: who jumped, when, and in which ship. With the connection's status and size, the jump log helps you judge how much mass a wormhole has left before you send more ships through.

Connection window​

Select a connection on the map to open the Connection window (see Map panels).

The left side summarizes the connection:

FieldDescription
SizeSize of the wormhole: Small, Medium, Large or XLarge
StatusCurrent status of the wormhole (see Wormhole status)
First JumpDate, time, character and ship of the first recorded jump
Last JumpDate, time, character and ship of the most recent jump

The right side lists every recorded jump:

ColumnDescription
DateDate of the jump (EVE time)
TimeTime of the jump (EVE time)
CharacterCharacter who jumped
ShipShip used, or No ship used if the connection was created manually

How jumps are recorded​

Tracked jumps​

When a tracked character jumps through a wormhole, WHMapper records the jump with the ship flown at that moment (see Pilot tracking).

Every jump through the same connection is added to the log, and First Jump / Last Jump are updated (see the two jumps in the first screenshot).

Manual connections​

When you create a connection by hand on the map, no ship is recorded: the log shows No ship used.

Wormhole status​

Status shows the mass status of the wormhole. When the wormhole is at end of life, its EOL status is shown next to it. Both are set from the connection menu on the map (see Editing a connection).

StatusColourMeaning
NormalGreenMass not reduced
ReducedOrangeMass reduced: a large ship or several ships went through
VergeRedAbout to collapse
EOL -4hPurpleEnd of life, less than about 4 hours left
EOL -1hPurpleEnd of life, less than about 1 hour left
